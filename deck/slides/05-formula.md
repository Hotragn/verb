## So put a number on it.

```formula
              R x H x u
        VB = -----------
                   c
```

<div class="cols">
<div>
<ul>
<li><strong>R</strong> people genuinely qualified to judge it</li>
<li><strong>H</strong> hours a week each has for reviewing</li>
</ul>
</div>
<div>
<ul>
<li><strong>u</strong> share of those hours that survives the calendar</li>
<li><strong>c</strong> hours to genuinely check one decision</li>
</ul>
</div>
</div>

<p class="small"><strong>VB</strong> is how many decisions of one kind you can
genuinely review per week. Compare it against how many you are producing.</p>

@notes

Four inputs, and you can get three of them from a spreadsheet this afternoon.

Be honest about R: it is not everyone with the approve button, it is the people
whose sign-off you would defend afterwards. That distinction usually cuts the
number by half and it is the first uncomfortable moment.

The fourth input, c, is the one nobody has. Flag it now and come back to it,
because it is the whole talk in one letter.

Do not derive anything. It is a division. If somebody wants the derivation they
can read the repo.
