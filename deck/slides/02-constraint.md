## For thirty years, the scarce thing was getting the work done.

<p>Somebody had to write the status report, chase the risk owners, rebuild the
forecast after the change request landed. Capacity meant counting the people who
could produce those things.</p>

<p><strong>That is over.</strong> An agent produces a defensible status pack for
forty projects in the time it takes you to open the file.</p>

<div class="margin"><b>Where this comes from</b><br>
Not a prediction. This is what teams running agents in delivery are reporting
right now.</div>

@notes

Ask for a show of hands: who has an AI tool writing something that used to take a
person an afternoon. Most hands go up. Leave the hands up for a beat.

Then the turn: everyone measured the win in hours saved on production. Nobody
measured what happened next.

Do not rush this slide. It is the only one where the audience is entirely
comfortable, and the rest of the talk depends on them agreeing with it.
