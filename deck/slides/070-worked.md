## Six people. Twenty-one decisions.

```formula
  VB = (6 x 8 x 0.55) / 1.25  =  21 a week
```

<p class="small">Six people qualified to review a schedule re-baseline, eight
review hours each, of which 55 percent survives the calendar, and an honest check
takes an hour and a quarter.</p>

@notes

Walk the arithmetic out loud once, slowly. Six times eight is forty-eight, times
0.55 is twenty-six hours, divided by an hour and a quarter is twenty-one.

Then the number that matters: forty-nine. Say it as a sentence, not a figure.
Forty-nine times a week, somebody signs their name to a decision they did not
have the capacity to check.

Nobody in that team is doing anything wrong. That is worth repeating.

If asked whether these numbers are real: no, they are illustrative and the repo
says so on the same page it prints them. Do not oversell.
